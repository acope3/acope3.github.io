I recently began contributing to a science communication newsletter [Grads Gone Rogue (GGR)](https://sites.google.com/view/grads-gone-rogue/home). You can find my first article covering Scopes "Monkey" Trial Centennial Symposium held at Vanderbilt University [here](https://sites.google.com/view/grads-gone-rogue/newsletter/august-2025/cope-august). I provide an overview of the *The State of Tennessee v. John Thomas Scopes*, more famously known as the Scopes "Monkey" Trial, and describe how it remains relevant to the current political and socal landscape, particularly in regards to science. Going forward, I plan to write articles covering evolutionary biology and its many common misconceptions (even among scientists). While you're over at GGR, be sure to check out the other articles!
